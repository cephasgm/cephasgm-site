Frequently asked questions

If your question isn't here, ask us directly — we answer every one.

What happens after the 14-day free trial?
Your account converts to your chosen tier at the end of the trial. No payment is required upfront — we'll email you three days before the trial ends with a link to add billing. If you do nothing, your account reverts to read-only mode rather than being deleted, so you never lose your data.
Can I switch plans later?
Yes, at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately with prorated billing. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. You never pay for a plan you're not using.
What counts as an "active project"?
Any project you've created that hasn't been archived. Archived projects don't count toward your limit — you can archive freely and restore later without losing data.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Every read and write is validated server-side through Firebase Security Rules with role-based access control. Client data is isolated per organisation — a client can only access their own projects. Audit logs record every change immutably. See our Architecture page for the full technical detail.
How does white-labeling work?
White-labeling is included in the Enterprise tier. You provide your logo, brand colours, and custom domain, and we configure the portal to serve entirely under your brand. Your clients never see the Cephas GM name. Setup takes 3–5 business days.
Can I pay annually?
Yes — annual billing saves you 17%, equivalent to two months free. Toggle the billing switch at the top of the page to see annual pricing. We accept all major credit cards and can arrange wire transfer for Enterprise customers.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits or education?
Yes. Non-profit organisations, educational institutions, and registered social enterprises receive 30% off any tier. Email info@cephasgm.org with proof of status and we'll apply the discount.
What if I need a feature that isn't listed?
Tell us. Custom modules, industry-specific workflows, and integrations are available on Enterprise. We've built bespoke systems for water-energy, OHS, ERP, and hardware-AI use cases — chances are we can adapt the platform to yours. Check our roadmap first to see if it's already planned.
Can I cancel at any time?
Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. When you cancel, your account stays active until the end of your paid period, then reverts to read-only. You can export all your data as CSV at any point before or after cancellation.
Do you offer volume pricing for consultancies?
Yes. If you're white-labeling the portal for multiple clients, we offer volume pricing starting at 10 organisations. Email admin@cephasgm.org with your expected client count and we'll send a custom quote within 48 hours.

Not sure which tier fits?

Tell us what you're building and we'll recommend the right plan — or design a custom one. Every conversation is with a human engineer, not a sales bot.
